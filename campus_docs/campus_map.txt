CAMPUS ORIENTATION
The Main Gate is on the north side of campus, facing Campus Road. Visitors should register at the Security Desk beside the gate.
The Central Courtyard is the main pedestrian landmark and connects the academic buildings.

BUILDINGS AND LOCATIONS
Admin Block: East of the Central Courtyard. Student Services and the Registrar's Office are on the ground floor.
Library: West of the Central Courtyard. The main entrance faces the courtyard; quiet study rooms are on the second floor.
Engineering Block: South of the Central Courtyard. The IoT Lab is in Room 204 and the Robotics Lab is in Room 206.
Computing Block: Southwest of the Central Courtyard. Computer Lab 301 is on the third floor.
Science Block: Southeast of the Central Courtyard. The Chemistry Lab is in Room 118.
Campus Bus Stop: Just inside the Main Gate, on the east side of the entrance road.

WALKING DIRECTIONS
From the Main Gate, walk south along the entrance road to reach the Central Courtyard.
From the courtyard, walk east to the Admin Block, west to the Library, south to the Engineering Block, or southeast to the Science Block.
The Computing Block is southwest of the courtyard, beyond the Engineering Block.
