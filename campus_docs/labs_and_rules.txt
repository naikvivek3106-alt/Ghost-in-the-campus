LAB LOCATIONS AND HOURS
IoT Lab: Engineering Block, Room 204. Open Monday through Friday, 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM.
Robotics Lab: Engineering Block, Room 206. Access is by instructor approval and advance booking.
Computer Lab 301: Computing Block, third floor. Open Monday through Friday, 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM, except during scheduled classes or maintenance.
Chemistry Lab: Science Block, Room 118. Access is limited to supervised practical sessions.

ACCESS AND CONDUCT
Carry a valid campus identity card and show it when requested by staff.
Sign in at the lab desk before using equipment. Follow the instructor's safety briefing and posted procedures.
Do not eat or drink in laboratories. Keep walkways and emergency exits clear.
Wear the protective equipment specified for the activity. Do not use damaged equipment; report it to the lab supervisor.
Do not remove equipment, chemicals, components, or records from a laboratory without written authorization.
Visitors may enter a laboratory only when accompanied by an authorized staff member.
Report injuries, spills, equipment failures, or other emergencies to the lab supervisor immediately.

BOOKING
Book the IoT Lab through the Engineering Department office at least one working day in advance.
Robotics Lab sessions require instructor approval before the room is reserved.
Scheduled teaching sessions take priority over open lab use.
